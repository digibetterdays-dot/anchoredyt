# Bonus Chapter Channel: Selected Topics

| # | Topic | Potential | Why it works |
|---|-------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Why American eggs live in the fridge and British eggs don't | Extremely high | US vs UK comparisons generate debates. Viewers will share their country's practices and challenge each other. |
| 2 | What if Yellowstone erupted? | Extremely high | A major American landmark, catastrophic possibilities, and enormous public curiosity. |
| 3 | Why school buses are that exact yellow | Extremely high | Almost every American recognizes the color. A familiar childhood object with a surprising historical explanation. |
| 4 | Why 911 is the emergency number | Extremely high | Every American knows the number, but relatively few know its history. Highly relevant and educational. |
| 5 | What if a 1-km asteroid hit New York? | Extremely high | Combines a recognizable city, disaster scenarios, and huge visual possibilities. |
| 6 | Why "AMBULANCE" is written backwards | Extremely high | Instantly recognizable, visually satisfying, and has a quick explanation. Excellent for Shorts. |
| 7 | What if the Cascadia fault went? | Very high | Serious regional consequences, especially for West Coast viewers. Strong emotional engagement. |
| 8 | Why stop signs are octagons | Very high | Something people see constantly but rarely question. The recognition-from-behind explanation is a great payoff. |
