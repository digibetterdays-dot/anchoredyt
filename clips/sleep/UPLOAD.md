## Do not say (titles, descriptions, replies)
"Melatonin switches your brain off"; "your brain is washed clean every night"; "you can train yourself to need less sleep"; a single reason why we sleep; "a family with the DEC2 gene" (say "a mother and daughter"); that the meta-analysis found a large effect (it was small); "nearly 50%" for the unwanted-thoughts study (press figure, not from the paper); a snoring prevalence figure; "your whole body is paralyzed" (most voluntary muscles; eyes, breathing and heart keep going).
