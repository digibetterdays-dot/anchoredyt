What would the
shaking feel like?
The last one
struck around nine
o'clock on a
late January night
in 1700.
The authors of
Oregon's own resilience
plan point out
the next one
could just as
easily shake a
mid-July morning,
with hundreds of
thousands of Oregonians
and visitors at
the coast.
No one can
predict it.
The key difference
from a typical
California quake is
how long it
lasts.
State geologists in
Oregon have said
shaking could last
several minutes.
Some of their
briefings have said
three to five.
Several minutes.
That's long enough
to be frightening,
long enough to
damage buildings,
and long enough
that you'd want
to know exactly
what to do.
