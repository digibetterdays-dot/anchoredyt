Illustration
Every school bus in America
is the same yellow.
Before 1939, there was
no national standard.
Frank Cyr, a professor at
Teachers College, Columbia,
brought officials and engineers
together to write standards.
One big question: color.
Lemon yellow to deep orange.
The group picked one:
National School Bus Chrome.
Visible from far away.
Black lettering stands out.
Cyr reportedly thought it looked
more orange than yellow.
A standard, chosen on purpose,
more than eighty years ago.
