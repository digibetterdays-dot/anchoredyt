Every detail
what a painter might have left out
Now a machine
could take a
likeness in minutes,
at a fraction
of the price,
and with a
precision no hand
could match.
And there was
a second shock,
for the sitters
themselves.
A painted portrait
could flatter.
A painter could
soften wrinkles,
adjust a nose,
straighten a posture,
and present a
person the way
the conventions of
beauty said they
should look.
The camera had
no such manners.
It recorded what
was there,
including the details
an artist might
have chosen to
leave out.
