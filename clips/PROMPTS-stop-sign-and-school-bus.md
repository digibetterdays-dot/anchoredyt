# Image and video prompts: two Shorts

You generate; I assemble in HyperFrames (voiceover, captions, timing). Save files exactly as named into the project's `assets/` folder (or upload them in chat and tell me the shot number).

**Style for every shot (paste at the end of each prompt):**
`flat editorial paper-cut illustration, soft grain, muted palette with one strong accent color, clean shapes, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, centered subject, empty space at top and bottom for captions`

**Honesty rule:** these are illustrations. Do not ask for "vintage photo", "archival", "documentary" or "1939 photograph" looks. I add an "illustration" label where a real event is shown.

**Video clips:** generate image-to-video from the matching still. Ask for 5 seconds, slow gentle motion only (parallax, slow push-in, small movements). I trim and loop to fit the narration. Name them `shotNN.mp4`; stills `shotNN.png`.
