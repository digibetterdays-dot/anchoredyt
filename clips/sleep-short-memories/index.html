Your brain needs
sleep not just
to rest,
but to process
what you've learned.
And sleep doesn't
only help you
hold on to
memories.
It may also
help you keep
unwanted ones at
bay.
In one study,
people who stayed
awake all night
found it much
harder to push
unpleasant images out
of their minds
than people who
had slept.
And even when
they managed it,
the thoughts were
more likely to
come back.
